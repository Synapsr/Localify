# Abuse Controls, and Shared Cookies Until the Public Suffix List

Opening public URLs to anyone who pays brings strangers under `localify.fyi`. Localify answers with controls on every public URL, and accepts one risk it cannot remove yet. This amends ADR 0022, which relied on everyone being invited, and ADR 0021, whose links that carry the password signed a browser in without a click.

**Warning page.** The first time a browser opens a public host, the Worker shows a page before any app content and before the password. The page says the content is a development preview served from someone's computer, names the owner's GitHub login, and tells the visitor not to enter credentials or payment details for another site. It covers protected URLs too: otherwise an attacker could hand out a link that carries the password and signs visitors in directly. Continuing sets a signed cookie for that host, valid 7 days. Only top-level navigations get the page. Assets, API calls, HMR, WebSockets and webhooks never see it, so apps and integrations keep working. A link that carries the password now waits for that one click.

**Moderation.** Every page the Worker serves links to `abuse@localify.dev`. An operator can take a URL down: it shows a removal page, its owner cannot bring it back, and its slug is never given out again. An operator can also ban an account (ADR 0024). Both run from one admin script against D1 and take effect within the 10-second cache of the public path.

**Traffic limits.** A per-owner limit, up to 2,000 requests a minute across all their URLs, absorbs a page that loads its hundreds of Vite modules at once, and caps what one account costs in Worker requests. A Cloudflare rate-limiting rule on `localify.fyi` limits each client IP before the Worker runs, because a 429 sent by the Worker is still a billed request.

**The accepted risk.** `localify.fyi` is not on the Public Suffix List yet. The list asks for more than two years of registration left and a service with thousands of users, and `localify.fyi` has neither today. Until it is listed, every public URL shares one registrable domain. A tenant's script can therefore set a cookie on `Domain=localify.fyi`, which browsers then send to every other tenant's app. The Worker can strip `Domain` from `Set-Cookie` headers, but not from cookies set by script. Localify's own cookies are host-only `__Host-` cookies and are safe. An app that reads ordinary cookies can be sent planted values, or receive enough of them to break its requests.

We accept this until the listing because every tenant has paid with a card, is known by a GitHub account, can be banned, and sits behind the warning page. Public URLs serve development previews, not production apps. `localify.fyi` gets renewed so that more than two years remain. It is submitted to the list once it qualifies, with the `_psl` TXT record the list requires. If abuse shows up before then, sign-up goes back to `allowlist` (ADR 0024). The terms tell users not to rely on cookies to isolate one public URL from another.

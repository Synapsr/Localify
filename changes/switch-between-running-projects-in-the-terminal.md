## Switch between your running projects in the terminal

A new sidebar lists only the projects and worktrees with something running, in the same
order as the projects screen, and the tabs show the processes of the one you select. A
red dot warns you when a service stops in the background, and `+` starts a service or
opens a shell on any other project. Background processes also keep their real terminal
width, so their output no longer wraps after a few columns.

## Check and control another machine's services from a terminal or an agent

Add `--machine <name>` to `services status`, `logs`, `dev`, `stop` and `restart` to act on
another of your machines, as the app's machine switcher does. Coding agents get the same
through `list_machines` and a `machine` argument on the service tools.

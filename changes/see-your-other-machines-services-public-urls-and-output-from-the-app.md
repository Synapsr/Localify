## See your other machines' services, public URLs and output from the app

Pick another Mac in the switcher next to Projects and see its projects as it sees them:
which services run and who started them, whether their ports answer, and their public
URLs, which open in one click. Open a service's output to read and search it live, as
plain text. Pair machines from the switcher or Settings → Remote access by confirming the
same 6-digit code on both, and choose there whether this Mac shares its projects and
output, which stays off until you turn it on. Needs Localify Cloud Pro.

## Let Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor do everything Localify does

Connect your coding agents with `localify agent install`, or **Connect agents** in
Settings → AI agents. They get every action of the app and the command line, from
starting services to managing domains, and always the ones of the version you have
installed. This replaces the Localify skill.

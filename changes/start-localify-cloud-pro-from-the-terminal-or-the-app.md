## Start Localify Cloud Pro from the terminal or the app

`localify subscribe` opens the checkout for Localify Cloud Pro, with its 7-day free
trial, and `localify billing` manages the subscription. `localify whoami` and
Settings → Localify Cloud show your plan, its dates and how much of it you use, and
a Mac whose public URLs cannot be served now says why and what to do.

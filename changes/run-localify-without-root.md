## Run Localify with nothing running as root

Localify on your Mac now asks for an administrator's password once, at install. After
that it runs under your own account and still starts at boot on ports 80 and 443.
Updates no longer ask for your password. When you want HTTPS in browsers, click Trust
HTTPS in the app or run `localify trust`, and macOS asks you to confirm once.

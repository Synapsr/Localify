## Buy more machines and URLs from the app or the terminal

On Localify Cloud Pro, Settings → Localify Cloud now has Add capacity: pick how many
serving machines or packs of 10 public URLs you need, see the monthly price, and finish
the checkout in your browser. In a terminal, `localify subscribe --machines 2` or
`localify subscribe --url-packs 1` does the same and waits for the new limit. When you hit
a limit, Localify names the command that raises it.

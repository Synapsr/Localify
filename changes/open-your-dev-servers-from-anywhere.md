## Open your dev servers from anywhere, behind a password

Sign in with GitHub, then give a project domain a public URL such as
`https://journal-a8f3.localify.fyi`, to open it from your laptop or phone on
another network, with hot reload working. Each URL asks for a password unless you
open it to everyone, and a wildcard URL reaches every service and branch of a
project. Use `localify public-url`, the Public URL section of each domain in the
app, or your coding agent. Accounts are invite-only for now.

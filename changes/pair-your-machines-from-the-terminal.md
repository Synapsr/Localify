## Pair your machines from the terminal

`localify machines` lists the other computers signed in to your Localify Cloud account,
with whether each one is online and paired. `localify machines pair <machine>` pairs this
one with another: both show the same 6-digit code, and you confirm it on each, so even
Localify Cloud cannot slip in a machine of its own. What paired machines may see or do here
stays off until you turn it on with `localify machines settings`. Remote access needs
Localify Cloud Pro; seeing a machine's projects and output from another one comes next.
